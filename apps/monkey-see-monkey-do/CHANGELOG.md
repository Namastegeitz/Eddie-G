# Monkey See, Monkey Do · What's new

Written for players. The newest version is at the top.

## 1.1.0 (build 2) · 27 November 2026

**New**
- Play with a game controller: shoulder buttons for the arms, triggers for the
  legs, Y for Head Bob, A for Flair. The D-pad works too, and in the menus you
  can move around with the D-pad, choose with A and go back with B. The buttons
  on screen show your controller's button names.
- Choose your own keyboard keys for each move (Settings > Controls > Keyboard keys).
- New graphics choices in Settings > Display: Medium, Sharpness (100%, 85%, 70%)
  and Frame rate (Max, 60, 30 to save battery).
- Settings > Help and privacy > Report a problem: your version, device and any
  recent errors, ready to copy into a bug report.
- On the web: when a new version is ready, a small bar offers to reload.

**Smoother**
- New maps open without a pause: the next map gets ready while you read your
  results (Settings > Display > Get maps ready ahead).
- Auto graphics keeps an eye on things while you play, and lowers the graphics
  one step if your device starts to struggle (for example when it gets warm).
- If your device resets its graphics (it can happen after the screen sleeps),
  the game pauses and carries on instead of freezing.
- Lush new jungle: tall trees with real-looking bark and thousands of leaves,
  ferns and a misty forest in the distance.
- On a computer or a tablet held sideways, the buttons move to the sides and the
  monkey gets bigger.
- The monkey dances more smoothly between moves.

**Gentler**
- With Reduce motion on, the disco floor flashes more softly.

**Phone apps**
- After a good level, the app may ask for a rating using the store's own box
  (never on the first day, at most 3 times a year; can be switched off).
- Crash reports are built in but switched off. If they are ever switched on,
  the privacy policy says exactly what they contain, and Settings has a switch.

## 1.0.0 (build 1) · November 2026

First beta: 40 levels in 4 worlds, take-turns rounds, Endless and Free Dance,
100 skins and a wardrobe of gear, an optional Shop in the phone apps (everything
can also be earned by playing), and a PC edition with no purchases.
