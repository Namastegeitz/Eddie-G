# Age-rating answers

Honest answers for every store's rating questions. Expected result
everywhere: **4+ / Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0**.

What's actually in the game, so you can check each answer:

- A cartoon monkey dances. On a mistake it stumbles and sees cartoon stars,
  then scratches its head. Nobody is hurt; there is no fighting.
- Costume skins include a cartoon vampire, skeleton and pumpkin (cute, not
  scary). No horror scenes, blood or jump scares.
- No words or images of a sexual, drug, alcohol, tobacco or gambling nature.
  No random prize boxes.
- No chat, no other players, no user-made content, no web browser, no ads.
- Mobile apps: optional in-app purchases (packs, bundles, skin sets) behind a
  grown-up question. The PC edition and web version have none.
- No data collected; no location; no account.

## Steam (content survey)

Tick none of the mature content boxes. "Frequent violence or gore", "General
mature content", "Sexual content", "Adult only": all **No**.

## Google Play: target audience

Suggested age groups: **5 and under, 6-8, 9-12, 13-15, 16-17, 18+** (all).
Including under-13s means Google's Families policy applies. The game already
meets it: no ads, no data collected, no outside links in play, and a grown-up
question before every purchase.
