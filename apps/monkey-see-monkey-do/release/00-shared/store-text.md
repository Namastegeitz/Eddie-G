### apple-description (limit 4000)
```
Watch the monkey dance. Copy the moves. Keep the beat.

Monkey See, Monkey Do is a rhythm memory game for the whole family. A cheerful 3D monkey shows you a little dance on the beat. Then it's your turn: tap Left Arm, Right Arm, Head Bob, Left Leg, Right Leg or Flair in the same order before the timer runs out. Get it right and the monkey cheers. Slip up and it stumbles, rubs its head, and lets you try the same dance again.

40 LEVELS, 4 MAPS
- A friendly start: 2-3 moves at a slow tempo while you learn.
- Then longer routines, faster tempos and combos where you tap two moves at once.
- Twists: build-up rounds, speed-up rounds and backwards rounds.
- Every level moves to a new map: jungle clearing, beach at sunset, temple disco and a party on the moon.
- Four worlds with a showdown at the end of each.

ENDLESS, YOUR MOVE! AND FREE DANCE
Endless keeps adding one move per round until you miss. Your Move! is a mini game where you take turns: copy the monkey's dance, add a move of your own, and watch it copy you. Free Dance has no timer and no score: just make the monkey dance.

DRESS UP
100 skins, from cocoa fur to galaxy, lava and solid gold, plus 88 pieces of gear: hats, glasses, capes, wings, jetpacks, microphones, rocket boots and sparkly auras. Save up to six outfits. Every skin and item can be earned by playing.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
- Timer settings (Normal, Relaxed, Off), slower game speeds and an Assist mode with hints.
- Every move has its own colour, shape, note and button position.
- Rhythm calibration for Bluetooth headphones.
- A "How it works" screen shows every rule, timer and price.

FAIR AND PRIVATE
No ads. No accounts. No tracking: the game collects no data. Optional packs of bananas and golden bananas are available, but you never need them. A grown-up question comes before every purchase, and you can set a monthly limit.
```
