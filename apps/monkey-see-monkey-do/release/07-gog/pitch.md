# GOG submission pitch (draft)

Paste into GOG's "Submit your game" form. Edit the bracketed parts.

**Title:** Monkey See, Monkey Do
**Genre:** Rhythm / memory / family
**Platforms:** Windows (macOS and Linux available)
**Price:** [$4.99] one-time, no in-game purchases
**Release:** [planned date] on [Steam / itch.io first, if so]

**One line:** Watch a cheerful 3D monkey dance, then copy its moves on the
beat, in a family rhythm memory game that's fully DRM-free and offline.

**Why it fits GOG**
- Fully DRM-free: no launcher, account, internet or tracking. Saves stay on
  the player's computer.
- Single-player, complete at launch: 40 levels across four worlds, Endless,
  Free Dance, 100 skins and 88 pieces of gear, all earned by playing.
- No ads, no microtransactions in the PC edition.
- Family-friendly (4+), with accessibility options: timer settings, slower
  speeds, Assist mode, rhythm calibration, colour plus shape plus sound for
  every move.

**What's distinctive**
- "Take turns" duets: copy the monkey's move, add one of your own, and the
  monkey copies you.
- Each level moves to a new map (jungle, beach, temple disco, moon).
- A "How it works" screen documents every rule and number in the game.
