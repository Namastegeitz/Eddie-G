# Notes for Apple App Review (paste into "App Review Information > Notes")

```
Monkey See, Monkey Do is a rhythm memory game: the monkey dances a short routine, then the player copies it with the six buttons.

No login is needed. To try everything quickly:
- Play: tap Play, then level 1 (guided). Levels 2 and 3 are "take turns" rounds where the player also adds a move.
- In-app purchases: Shop button on the title screen. A grown-up question (a multiplication sum) appears before every purchase; this is the parental gate. Settings > Purchases has a monthly limit and Restore Purchases.
- Every item sold can also be earned by playing (Wardrobe shows each item's play goal or banana price).

The app collects no data, has no ads, no third-party SDKs other than StoreKit, and makes no network requests of its own.
```
