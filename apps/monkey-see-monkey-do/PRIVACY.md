# Privacy policy: Monkey See, Monkey Do

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Monkey See, Monkey Do is a game that runs entirely on your device.

- **We collect nothing.** The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
  tracking. It doesn't send any data anywhere. The Android app doesn't even
  ask for internet access.
- **What stays on your device:** your progress (levels, stars, bananas,
  wardrobe), your settings, play statistics and a list of bananas earned and
  spent. It's stored in the app's or browser's local storage on your device.
  You can view it, copy it or delete it in Settings > Your data. Uninstalling
  the app or clearing the browser's site data removes it.
- **Device features:** vibration (if you turn it on) and keeping the screen
  awake while you play. The game doesn't use the camera, microphone, location
  or contacts.
- **Children:** the game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal
  information from anyone, including children.
- **Purchases:** there are none. Bananas are earned by playing and can't be bought.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
