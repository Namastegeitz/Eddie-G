# Privacy policy: Monkey See, Monkey Do

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Monkey See, Monkey Do is a game that runs entirely on your device.

- **We collect nothing.** The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
  tracking. It doesn't send any data anywhere. The Android app doesn't even
  ask for internet access (its only store permission is Google Play Billing,
  for the Shop).
- **What stays on your device:** your progress (levels, stars, bananas,
  wardrobe), your settings, play statistics, a list of bananas earned and
  spent, and a list of Shop purchases (product, price, date). It's stored in the app's or browser's local storage on your device.
  You can view it, copy it or delete it in Settings > Your data. Uninstalling
  the app or clearing the browser's site data removes it.
- **Device features:** vibration (if you turn it on) and keeping the screen
  awake while you play. The game doesn't use the camera, microphone, location
  or contacts.
- **Children:** the game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal
  information from anyone, including children.
- **Purchases:** the iPhone and Android apps have an optional Shop. Payments are
  made in the App Store or Google Play and are covered by Apple's or Google's
  privacy policy. The game never sees your name, card or store account; the
  store tells the app only which product was bought, and the app keeps that
  record on your device. Nothing can only be had by paying: everything can be
  earned by playing. A grown-up question comes before every purchase (on by
  default), and Settings > Purchases has an optional monthly limit. The web
  version sells nothing.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
