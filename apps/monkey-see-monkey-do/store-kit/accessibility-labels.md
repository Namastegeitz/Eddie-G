# Apple Accessibility Nutrition Labels: suggested answers

App Store Connect > the app > Accessibility. Voluntary today; Apple says they
will become required. Claim a feature only if the **common tasks** (start a
level, play it, see results, change settings) work with it. Checked against
the 1.1.0 build.

| Label | Claim? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| VoiceOver | **No** | Buttons are labelled and announcements exist, but copying a dance relies on seeing the monkey |
| Voice Control | **No** | Not tested end to end |
| Larger Text | **No** | Text doesn't follow the system text size |
| Dark Interface | **Yes** | The game is dark-themed everywhere |
| Differentiate Without Color Alone | **Yes** | Every move has its own icon, name, position and key, not just a colour |
| Sufficient Contrast | **Yes, after a check** | Cream text on dark green; verify the smallest notes with a contrast checker before claiming |
| Reduced Motion | **Yes** | Settings > Display > Reduce motion (and the system setting) removes camera shake, cuts confetti and calms the disco floor |
| Captions | **Yes** | Move names show during Watch (Settings > Display > Move names during Watch); no spoken story or dialogue |
| Audio Descriptions | **No** | |

Google Play has no equivalent form; mention Reduce motion, Assist mode,
slower speed, relaxed timer, controller and custom keys in the description
if space allows.
