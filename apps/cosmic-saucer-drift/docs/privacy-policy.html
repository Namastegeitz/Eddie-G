Cosmic Saucer Drift - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

Cosmic Saucer Drift does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases in this version.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (Stardust, unlocks, upgrades, best times, time-trial ghosts, achievements and settings) in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device. You can erase it in Settings > Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The Android app asks only for permission to vibrate, used for haptic feedback during races. You can turn vibration off in Settings. If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the motion sensor while you race; those readings are used only to steer and are not stored.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing what is collected, by whom, and how to opt out.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer at the email address listed on the game's Google Play page.
