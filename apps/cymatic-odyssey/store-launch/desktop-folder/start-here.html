Open the app

The app runs in any browser, and installs to a phone's home screen. The folders next to this one hold everything else:

  • 2 Manual and guides: the 77-page manual (PDF), the sourcing guide, and the step-by-step guide for Google Play and the App Store.
  • 3 Print files: the 30 style kits (STL and SVG), Bambu Studio plates (3MF), Blender-enhanced parts, hardware, inserts and dipper feet.
  • 4 Gobo library: every printable gobo, one zip per family.
  • 5 Show videos: the 15 show previews (simulations).
  • 6 Android app: the installable APK (copy it to an Android phone and open it).

Every pattern picture and video is a simulation until a real chamber is filmed. An art and relaxation experience, not a medical device. To refresh the folder later, run the same command again: it only downloads what's missing.
