Cymatic Light Chamber

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026. Applies to the Cymatic Light Chamber web app and its Android and iOS apps.

In short: the app collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • That you accepted the safety notice (so it isn't shown every time).
  • Your pattern atlas, if you log patterns in the console (frequency, mode, depth and the label you choose).

These live in your browser's or app's local storage. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, deletes them. You can export the atlas yourself as a CSV file.

What the app loads from the internet

The app works offline. When you open the manual, a download, a show video or the Android installer, it fetches that file from GitHub Pages (github.io), which hosts the app. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when serving files; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive those logs.

Microphone, camera, location, contacts

The app doesn't use them. It plays sound through your device's audio output only.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health

This is an art and relaxation experience, not a medical device. It doesn't collect health or heart-rate data. If future versions add optional heart-rate features, that data will stay on your device unless you explicitly opt in, and this policy will be updated before then.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: see the support page.
