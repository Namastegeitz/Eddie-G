## What to look for in a glass vessel

- **Clear, thin and flat-bottomed.** Patterned, textured or very thick bottoms smear the caustics. Borosilicate (Pyrex-type) survives heat best.
- **Shape = look.** Round, square and hexagonal dishes give different wall-mode patterns at low frequencies, the same idea as the printed inserts.
- **Fits the platform.** The recess is 152 mm. For other sizes, change `dish_od` in `tools/odyssey/build.py` and regenerate.
- **Art glass and dichroic sheets** make strong colour filters. Put them on the gobo carrier, not in the water.
- Hand-blown pieces are beautiful but uneven. Use them as filters or display pieces, and keep a lab dish as the water vessel.
- Call any shop before driving; hours and stock in these listings may be out of date.
