## 4. Two-frequency recipes (quasipatterns & superlattices)

From the literature (Edwards & Fauve 1993; Kudrolli, Pier & Gollub 1998; Arbell & Fineberg 2002). Mix two sines with integer ratio m:n into the same exciter. Exact thresholds depend on fluid depth and viscosity - water is low-viscosity, so expect to work near onset and sweep slowly.

| Ratio | Example (Hz) | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| 4:5 | 40 + 50 | 12-fold quasipattern (Edwards & Fauve) |
| 6:7 | 42 + 49 | superlattice / 12-fold, hexagon-in-hexagon (Kudrolli et al.) |
| 2:3 | 40 + 60 | hexagons and triangles instead of squares |
| 1:2 | 30 + 60 | hexagons favoured, phase-sensitive |
| 3:5 | 36 + 60 | rhombic / mixed patterns |
| 1 only | 60 | squares (low-viscosity single frequency) |
