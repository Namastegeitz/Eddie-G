# Market section

## The one-line pitch

**Real water, real physics, sound-driven geometry on any wall.** A sealed tabletop chamber that turns sound into living light patterns, with a themed show for every holiday and a calm mode for every space.

## What makes it different

| Common alternative | What it is | Where CYMATIC ODYSSEY differs |
|---|---|---|
| "Water ripple" effect lamps ($20-60) | A rotating textured glass disc in front of an LED, faking caustics | Real water responding to sound; geometry on demand; it changes with music and themes |
| 1960s liquid light shows | Overhead projectors with dishes of oil and dye, moved by hand | Same projection idea, but driven by tuned frequencies, repeatable, themeable, hands-free |
| Research cymatics instruments | Lab-grade imaging devices | A home and venue product: printable, affordable, with a story-driven show |
| Projectors with video loops | Pre-rendered content | Physical light: crisp caustics, real depth, never the same twice |

The **catalogue** (355 printable looks with documented maths and history), the **themes calendar** (40 holidays and observances) and the **voyages** (story-arc shows) are the content moat. They're cheap to extend and hard to copy well.

## Health and wellbeing: what you can and can't say

This is ambience, not medicine. In the US, health claims need "competent and reliable scientific evidence" (FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance, 2022). Claiming to treat, cure or prevent a condition can make the product a medical device in the FDA's eyes. Keep claims to relaxation, focus, mood and wonder, and cite the general evidence honestly.

| Idea | What the evidence supports | Safe wording |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow-paced breathing** (about 6 breaths/min) | Reviews link slow breathing with higher heart-rate variability, relaxation and lower self-reported anxiety (Zaccaro et al., *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 2018; Lehrer & Gevirtz, *Frontiers in Psychology*, 2014) | "Includes a breathing-paced visual mode to help you slow down." |
| **Water and nature-like visuals ("blue space")** | Being near water is associated with wellbeing in population studies (e.g. White et al., *Environmental Research*, 2020, narrative review) | "Brings the calm of moving water indoors." |
| **Fractal patterns** | Experiments by Richard Taylor and colleagues suggest mid-complexity fractals (dimension about 1.3-1.5, like clouds and trees) are preferred and may reduce physiological stress (e.g. Taylor, *Leonardo* 39(3), 2006) | "Natural fractal-style patterns that are easy on the eyes." |
| **Multi-sensory / sensory rooms** | Widely used in dementia and autism care; outcome evidence is mixed | "Designed for calm sensory spaces." Partner with professionals; no outcome claims. |
| **"Healing frequencies"** (432 Hz, Solfeggio 528 Hz "DNA repair", etc.) | No reliable evidence | **Don't claim.** You can offer them as musical tunings ("tuned to 432 Hz") without health language. |
| **Vibration therapy** | Some small studies on body-contact vibroacoustic therapy; this device doesn't touch the body | **Don't claim.** |

Wellbeing safeguards are selling points too: no flicker (high-frequency LED drivers), slow motion by design, sealed low-voltage chamber, quiet mode.

## Getting to market

1. **Prove the look:** film the chamber with 5 themes; post short clips.
2. **Maker launch:** release a free starter STL pack (10 gobos, 5 inserts) on Printables to build a community; sell the full pack and theme subscriptions.
3. **Pilot venues:** 3-5 local spas, studios and bars on a free 30-day loan in exchange for footage and testimonials.
4. **Kit, then assembled units:** small batches; get the certifications below before selling assembled electronics.
5. **Seasonal B2B:** sell holiday installs and rentals 6-8 weeks ahead using the themes calendar.
6. **Education:** a classroom kit + lesson plans (waves, symmetry, astronomy) for science centres and schools.

## Before selling hardware: compliance checklist

- **Power:** an external UL/ETL-listed 24 V supply (keeps mains out of the product).
- **EMC/radio:** FCC Part 15 (US); use a pre-certified Bluetooth/Wi-Fi module (the ESP32 module's FCC ID). CE/UKCA for EU/UK (EMC, LVD, RED), RoHS, WEEE.
- **LED photobiological safety:** IEC 62471 risk-group classification for the light source.
- **Water + electronics:** design for spills (IP rating on the electronics bay); test with a full dish tipped.
- **Labels and manuals:** flicker/photosensitivity notice, water handling, no-implant warning only if you ever include an arc device (don't).
- **Tesla coil:** not part of any sold product.
- **Product liability insurance;** California Prop 65 review of materials.
- **Name:** run a trademark search on "Cymatic Odyssey" before branding. Avoid other companies' trademarked cymatics product names.

## Risks and answers

| Risk | Answer |
|---|---|
| Cheap "ripple lamps" anchor the price low | Sell the experience: real water, sound sync, themes, the catalogue. Demo video side by side. |
| Water + electronics fear | Sealed chamber, drip tray, 24 V only, spill test in marketing |
| Novelty wears off at home | Monthly theme drops, voyages, new gobo packs |
| Venue staff turnover | One-button themes in the console; auto-schedule by date (next step) |
