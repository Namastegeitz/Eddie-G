# 15 light shows (10 minutes each, intertwined)

Every show ends with a 60-second bridge that becomes the next show's opening, and each borrows a guest style from the show six places ahead. Play one, or press *Continuous* in the console to run all 15 (150 minutes) as one loop. Drive mode switches automatically when the platform is on the left amp channel and the dipper speaker on the right (see BUILD_SPECS). Gobo and insert swaps are optional cues.
