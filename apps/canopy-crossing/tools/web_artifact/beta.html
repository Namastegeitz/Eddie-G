Canopy Crossing BETA

Hop real jungle animals across rivers, roads and dense canopy in 20 real rainforests. Thanks for testing!

Play in your browser

Works on iPhone and Android. Best held upright, sound on.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone and iPadYOUR PHONE

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Canopy Crossing from your home screen: it runs full screen, like an app.

There's no App Store version yet, so this is how to install it on an iPhone.

AndroidYOUR PHONE

Or without installing anything: open this page in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

What to try

  • Finish the tutorial, then a few Amazon levels. Watch for the giant red soldier ants hauling cheese.
  • When the leaves close in, tap the eye button to switch to first person and cross the canopy.
  • Spin the globe on the world map, and try Endless and the Daily Challenge.
  • Settings › Graphics: try High, Medium and Low, and switch on Show FPS.
