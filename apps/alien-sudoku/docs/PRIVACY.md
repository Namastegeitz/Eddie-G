## Information the game stores

Your progress, settings, statistics and achievements are stored **only on your
device**. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own, and we do not
receive this information. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset all progress"
in Settings deletes it.

## Advertising (iOS and Android apps)

The mobile apps are free and show ads from **Google AdMob**. To show and measure
ads, Google may collect and process:

- device identifiers such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID),
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- information about your device and the ads you see and interact with,
- diagnostic data.

Depending on where you live, the app asks for your consent before personalized
ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. On iOS, the app asks for permission
to track (App Tracking Transparency). You can change your choice at any time:

- in the game: Settings → *Ad privacy choices* (shown where required),
- on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking,
- on Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

How Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.
Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

The web and desktop versions of the game show no ads and send no data.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.
