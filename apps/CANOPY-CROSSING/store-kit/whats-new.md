## 0.9.0 (beta, November 2026)

**Short (Play / Apple):**
Sharper jungles everywhere, new graphics options (Ultra, frame rate, 3D
resolution), smoother screens from above, and the game now pauses when you
switch away.

**Long:**
- All 22 jungles look sharper: detailed grass, roads, rivers and leafy canopy, in both views.
- New graphics options: Ultra for strong computers, a 30/60/Max frame-rate choice, and first-person resolution.
- Smoother from above: the busiest screens draw far less work.
- Leaving the app mid-run now opens the pause menu.
- Fixed a memory leak on long play sessions.
- Optional crash reports help us fix problems (Settings > Send crash reports).
