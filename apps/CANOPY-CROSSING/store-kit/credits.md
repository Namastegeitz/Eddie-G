## AI-generated content (stores ask)

- **3D models**: first generated with an AI text-to-3D tool (Meshy), then
  processed for the game (polygon budgets, texture sizes). Declare on Steam's
  Content Survey and itch.io's AI disclosure ("Graphics").
- Everything else (code, music, sound, 2D art, text) was written or generated
  by our own code, not an AI art or music service. Code was written with
  Claude's help.
