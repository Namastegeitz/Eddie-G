## The short version

Canopy Crossing is a single-player game. You don't need an account, and the
game works offline. Your progress is saved on your own device. We don't
collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

## What is stored on your device

- **Your progress and settings** (animals, levels, Berries and Amber,
  settings) in the game's own storage on your device. It stays there unless
  you delete the game or use *Settings > Reset all progress*.
- **Gameplay events** (for example: a level started, a level finished, a
  purchase made, a setting changed) in a log file in the game's storage.
  [Today this log never leaves your device. If you later turn on analytics,

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by the store you bought from (Apple
App Store, Google Play, Amazon, Samsung). We never see your card or bank
details. The store tells the game which items you bought so it can unlock
them, and *Restore purchases* asks the store again after a reinstall.

## Ads

[If you ship with ads, keep this section and fill it in. If not, delete it.]
The game can show optional reward videos and, between runs, occasional ads,
provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your
device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address,
and information about the ads you see and tap. Personalised ads are **off**
by default; you can change this in *Settings > Personalised ads*. On iPhone
and iPad you'll only be asked to allow tracking if personalised ads are
turned on. Buying *Remove Ads* turns off the between-run ads. Google's
policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
