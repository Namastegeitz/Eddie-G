## Full description (Apple 4000, Google 4000; reuse for Steam 'About this game', GOG, itch.io)
_1822 / 4000 characters_

```
Hop, swim and leap your way across 22 of the world's great rainforests with 50 real animals, from a tiny poison dart frog to a silverback gorilla.

CROSS THE JUNGLE
Tap to hop, swipe to dodge. Ride floating logs and caiman backs across rivers, time your hops between trucks and charging cassowaries on busy trade roads, and slip past snakes, eagles, quicksand and crumbling waterfall ledges. Every level gets a little harder, so the first jungles are gentle and the later ones are a real challenge.

SEE IT THROUGH THEIR EYES
Switch between a bird's-eye view and first person at any time. Under the dense canopy the ground disappears from above, so drop into first person to see the logs, crocodiles and ants hauling cheese right in front of you.

50 ANIMALS, 50 ABILITIES
Every animal plays differently: the howler monkey swings on vines, the capybara floats, the jaguar dashes, the harpy eagle swoops, the chameleon vanishes. Level up their skills and find your favourite.

22 REAL RAINFORESTS, 220 LEVELS
From the Amazon and the Congo to Borneo, the Daintree, Madagascar and Puerto Rico's El Yunque, each jungle has its own animals, hazards, music and fun facts on a spinning globe. Clear a jungle and the next one opens.

FULL OF LIFE
Giraffes stroll and kangaroos bound down the trade roads, scarlet macaws fly overhead, bees work the fruit stalls, and now and then an animal turns and pulls a silly face at you.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Journey mode, Endless mode and a Daily Challenge. Calm, soothing music for every jungle. Plays offline. Colour-blind hazard markers, left-handed controls, reduce motion and a mini-map.

Canopy Crossing is free to play. It offers optional in-app purchases (animal packs, map packs, Amber, an Expedition Pass, Remove Ads) and optional reward videos; everything can also be earned by playing.
```
