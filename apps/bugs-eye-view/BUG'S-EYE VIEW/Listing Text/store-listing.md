## Full description (2,251 of 4,000 characters; Google Play, App Store, Steam, GOG, Epic, Microsoft, itch.io)
You are a bug. Everything else is enormous.

Bug's-Eye View is a stealth game seen from the floor. Pick a bug, slip into a
busy house and gather enough crumbs to escape - without being stomped,
swatted, sprayed, pounced on or vacuumed up.

EIGHT BUGS, EIGHT WAYS TO PLAY
Start as the cockroach - fast and tough - and unlock the fly, the spider, the
ant, the cricket, the ladybug, the firefly and the stink bug. Fly through the
air, swing on silk, chirp to throw your voice, shell up, glow, or clear the
room with a stink cloud.

A HOUSE THAT LIVES ITS DAY
Three big rooms - the kitchen, the living room and the backyard - follow a
daily routine. The cook makes breakfast and opens the fridge, the reader dozes
in the armchair, the gardener waters the flowers and throws the ball for the
dog. A cat and a dog play, nap and prowl. A robot vacuum hunts crumbs - and
you.

THEY HUNT YOU DIFFERENTLY
On the floor they stomp. Up on a table they lean over and slap with an open
hand. Out of reach they throw a slipper, a sponge or a ball of paper. Shadows
and warning rings show where a strike will land - move in time.

MAKE A DISTRACTION
Push a glass, a teacup or a potted plant off the counter, or tip over a row of
toy dominoes: when it crashes, everyone in the room stops and stares - and
that's your moment.

CLIMB EVERYTHING
Walls, table legs, curtains and fridges. Ride bounce pads up to high shelves,
hunt for hidden power-ups and gems, and fill a showcase with rare and
legendary treasures.

MINI MAPS AND ADD-ONS
Thirty small levels hide behind the rooms - inside the walls, under the sink,
in the basement and the attic, plus a secret crawl space. Earn credits, buy
add-ons in the Armory and load them into a six-chamber revolver: decoys,
shields, dashes, smoke bombs and a silk grapple.

WATCH YOUR STEP
Snap traps knock you out cold. Your health comes back if you lie low, and
crumbs and power-ups heal you - but two traps back to back will finish you.

FEATURES
- Third-person or true bug's-eye first-person view
- Touch, keyboard and mouse, and controller support
- Eight bugs, three rooms, thirty mini maps, a secret crawl space
- Twenty-four treasures to collect
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no data collected
- Plays offline
