# Screenshot and trailer shot list

Finished screenshots are in `../Store Assets/` (rendered from the real game by
`tools/release/make_store_assets.sh`). This list says what each store needs and
which shots to use, so a re-render covers everything.

## Screenshots (same six scenes everywhere)
1. **Kitchen** - the cook turns, a stomp's shadow closes in (the hook)
2. **Dominoes** - a row of toy dominoes falling: the crash distraction
3. **Slap** - a hand sweeping across the table at the bug
4. **Fridge** - the open fridge full of food (Meshy models)
5. **Trap** - knocked out by a snap trap
6. **Bug's-eye** - the first-person view

| Store / device | Size | Count | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steam, GOG, Epic, Microsoft, itch.io, Google Play phone/tablet | 1920 x 1080 | 5-6 | Screenshots/1920x1080 |
| App Store iPhone 6.9" | 2868 x 1320 | 3-10 | Screenshots/iPhone 6.9 inch |
| App Store iPhone 6.3" (required size) | 2622 x 1206 | 3-10 | Screenshots/iPhone 6.3 inch |
| App Store iPad 13" | 2752 x 2064 | 3-10 | Screenshots/iPad 13 inch |
| Google Play foldables (from Apr 2027) | inner screen, e.g. 2208 x 1840 | 3 + 1 phone | to render when needed |

## Trailer / app preview (30-60 s; App Store previews 15-30 s)
| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Bug's-eye: a giant foot slams down right in front of the camera |
| 3-8 s | Title card over the kitchen; the bug scurries under the table |
| 8-15 s | Push a teacup off the counter; everyone turns to the crash; the bug runs |
| 15-22 s | Fly over the living room; spider swings on silk; firefly glows |
| 22-30 s | Snap trap knock-out, the vacuum chase, escape through the goal |
| 30-35 s | Logo, platforms, "Sneak. Scavenge. Survive." |

Record at 1920 x 1080 / 60 fps on High or Ultra with the HUD visible (stores
want real gameplay). App Store previews: one per device size, recorded or
scaled to that size, no outside footage.
