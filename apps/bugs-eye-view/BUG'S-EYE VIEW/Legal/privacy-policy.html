Privacy policy

Bug's-Eye View

Effective 28 November 2026 (crash reports added) · Developer: Aethertek · Applies to the game on Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS, Linux and the web.
The short version: Bug's-Eye View does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The only thing it can ever send is an anonymous crash report, and only if you say yes.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your progress and settings (unlocked bugs and levels, crumbs, credits, add-ons, collected treasures, volume and control settings) are saved only on your own device: in the app's private storage on a phone or tablet, in your user folder on a computer, or in your browser's storage for the web version. We never receive them.
  • You can erase them at any time with Settings › Reset all progress in the game, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Crash reports (optional, off until you say yes)

The first time you open the game it asks whether it may send crash reports. Nothing is sent before you answer yes, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & about.

If you say yes and the game crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a report so the bug can be fixed. A report contains:

  • the device model, operating system and its version, and the game's version;
  • what went wrong (the error, where in the game's code it happened, and the game's own log of that session);
  • which room and which bug you were playing;
  • a random installation number created by the crash-reporting service, so repeat crashes on one device can be grouped. It is not linked to you.

Reports never contain your name, email, account, contacts, location, photos or anything you typed. They are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold or shared. They are processed for us by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) and, on Android, Firebase Crashlytics (Google), under their privacy terms, and are kept for at most 90 days.

What the game does not do

  • It collects no personal information: no name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • It doesn't go online while you play, except to send a crash report you allowed.
  • It reads and sends no device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • It includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • It shares or sells no data with anyone.
  • Turning crash reports off stops all sending; anything already sent is deleted within 90 days, or sooner if you ask at the contact address below.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Stores and platforms

The store you get the game from (Google Play, the App Store, Steam, GOG, itch.io, Epic Games Store, Microsoft Store) may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or buy the game. That is between you and the store.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online features are added, this page will be updated before the change ships and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the support email on the game's store page (being set up before release).
