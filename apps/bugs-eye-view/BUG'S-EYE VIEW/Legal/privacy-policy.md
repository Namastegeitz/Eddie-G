## The short version
Bug's-Eye View does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It
has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases,
and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

## What the game keeps, and where
- **Your progress and settings** (unlocked bugs and levels, crumbs, credits,
  add-ons, collected treasures, volume and control settings) are saved **only
  on your own device** - in the app's private storage on a phone or tablet, in
  your user folder on a computer, or in your browser's storage for the web
  version. We never receive them.
- You can erase them at any time: **Settings > Reset all progress** in the
  game, or uninstall the app / clear the site's data in your browser.

## What the game does not do
- No personal information is collected (no name, email, location, contacts,
  photos, microphone or camera).
- No device identifiers or advertising IDs are read or sent.
- No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software is included.
- No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information
from anyone, including children.

## Stores and platforms
The store you get the game from (Google Play, the App Store, Steam, GOG,
itch.io, Epic, Microsoft Store) may collect information under its own privacy
policy when you download or buy the game; that is between you and the store.
