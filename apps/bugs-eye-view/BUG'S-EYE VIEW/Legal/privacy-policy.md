## The short version
Bug's-Eye View does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It
has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
The only thing it can ever send is an anonymous crash report, and only if
you say yes.

## What the game keeps, and where
- **Your progress and settings** (unlocked bugs and levels, crumbs, credits,
  add-ons, collected treasures, volume and control settings) are saved **only
  on your own device** - in the app's private storage on a phone or tablet, in
  your user folder on a computer, or in your browser's storage for the web
  version. We never receive them.
- You can erase them at any time: **Settings > Reset all progress** in the
  game, or uninstall the app / clear the site's data in your browser.

## Crash reports (optional, off until you say yes)
The first time you open the game it asks whether it may send crash reports.
Nothing is sent before you answer yes, and you can switch it off at any time
in **Settings > Privacy & about**.

If you say yes and the game crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a
report so the bug can be fixed. A report contains:
- the device model, operating system and its version, and the game's version;
- what went wrong (the error, where in the game's code it happened, and the
  game's own log of that session);
- which room and which bug you were playing;
- a random installation number created by the crash-reporting service, so
  repeat crashes on one device can be grouped. It is not linked to you.

Reports never contain your name, email, account, contacts, location, photos
or anything you typed. They are not used for advertising or tracking and are
not sold or shared. They are processed for us by **Sentry** (Functional
Software, Inc.) and, on Android, **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google), under
their privacy terms, and are kept for at most 90 days.

## What the game does not do
- No personal information is collected (no name, email, location, contacts,
  photos, microphone or camera).
- It doesn't go online while you play, except to send a crash report you
  allowed.
- No device identifiers or advertising IDs are read or sent.
- No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software is included.
- No data is shared with or sold to anyone.
- Turning crash reports off stops all sending; anything already sent is
  deleted within 90 days, or sooner if you ask at the contact address below.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Stores and platforms
The store you get the game from (Google Play, the App Store, Steam, GOG,
itch.io, Epic, Microsoft Store) may collect information under its own privacy
policy when you download or buy the game; that is between you and the store.
