# Bug's-Eye View - changelog

Written for players. Newest first.

## 1.0.0 - upgrade of 28 Nov 2026
- **Your progress is safer:** saving can no longer wipe your progress if the
  game or device crashes mid-save (a backup is kept), and progress from
  older desktop versions moves over automatically.
- **Smoother first moments:** rooms prepare their graphics while loading,
  so new objects no longer stutter the first time you see them (the worst
  hitches are 40-74% shorter in our tests).
- **More graphics choices:** a new Ultra setting (sharper edges, finer
  textures, soft contact shadows), a frame-rate cap with a 120 fps option,
  and a render-scale setting.
- **Easier to read and play:** text size up to 200%, a reduced-motion switch
  (no screen shake, still menus), and the game pauses when you switch away.
- **Optional crash reports:** the game asks once; nothing is sent unless you
  say yes, and you can turn it off in Settings.

## 1.0.0 - 17 Nov 2026
- Every bug can push the potted plants; Privacy & about in Settings; first
  store-ready builds for every platform.
