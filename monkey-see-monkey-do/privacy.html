Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. Applies to the game on the web, iPhone, iPad, Android, and the PC edition.

We collect nothing

Monkey See, Monkey Do runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send any data anywhere. The Android app doesn't even ask for internet access (its only store permission is Google Play Billing, for the optional Shop).

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, bananas, wardrobe), your settings, play statistics, a list of bananas earned and spent, and a list of Shop purchases (product, price, date). It is stored in the app's or browser's local storage on your device.

You can view it, copy it or delete it in Settings > Your data. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data removes it.

Device features

Vibration (only if you turn it on) and keeping the screen awake while you play. The game doesn't use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

The iPhone and Android apps have an optional Shop. Payments are made in the App Store or Google Play and are covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy. The game never sees your name, card or store account: the store tells the app only which product was bought, and the app keeps that record on your device.

Nothing can only be had by paying: everything can be earned by playing. A grown-up question comes before every purchase (on by default), and Settings > Purchases has an optional monthly limit. The web version and the PC edition sell nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
