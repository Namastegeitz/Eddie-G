Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact

Tell the person who shared the game with you, or use the review or support link of the store you got it from.

There's no sound

Tap the screen once: phones only start game sound after a tap. On iPhone, also check Settings > Sound in the game: Play sound on silent decides what the silent switch does.

My taps are graded early or late

Bluetooth headphones add a delay. Open Settings > Rhythm calibration and tap along to the clicks: the game measures your delay and adjusts.

The game runs slowly

Settings > Graphics > Low. Auto also steps down by itself after a few seconds if your device struggles. Graphics never change timing or scoring.

Moving my progress to a new phone

Progress is saved on each device only. On the old device open Settings > Your data > Copy save code, send it to yourself, then paste it on the new device under Load save code.

Purchases (iPhone and Android apps)

Bundles and skin sets come back with Settings > Purchases > Restore. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from (Apple or Google). Every purchase is listed in Stats > Purchases. Everything can also be earned by playing.

Taking turns (levels 2 and 3)

Copy the monkey's move, then tap any move to add your own. There is no wrong move to add. The monkey copies you and adds another; copy the whole dance to clear the round.
