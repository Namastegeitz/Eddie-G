Alien Sudoku: Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Use the contact details on the game's store page.

How do I play?

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box holds each of the nine alien glyphs exactly once. Tap a cell, then a glyph. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates. Settings → How to play has a full guide.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset all progress.

I saw an inappropriate ad

In the game, open Settings → Report an ad. Say what the ad showed and when you saw it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required). On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads. See the privacy policy.

There's no sound

Check that the phone is not on silent and the volume is up, then open Settings → Test sound in the game.
