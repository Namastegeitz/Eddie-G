Alien Sudoku: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Alien Sudoku ("the game") is made by Eddie G. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress, settings, statistics, achievements, Alien Duel record and the name you may give the visitors are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics, and we do not receive any of this information. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset all progress" in Settings deletes it.

The Alien Duel rival is a computer opponent that runs entirely on your device. Nothing about your games is sent anywhere.

Advertising (iOS and Android apps only)

The phone and tablet apps are free and show ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device and the ads you see and interact with,
  • diagnostic information about the ads.

Depending on where you live, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. On iOS, the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). You can change your choice at any time:

  • in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required),
  • on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking,
  • on Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The web and desktop versions of the game show no ads. The only data they can send are crash reports (below).

Crash reports

If the game crashes or runs into an error, it can send a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. Reports go to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) from every version of the game and, in the iOS and Android apps, to Google Firebase Crashlytics.

A report contains the error and where in the game's code it happened, the app version, the platform, the device model and operating system version, the graphics setting, and (Crashlytics) a random installation ID. It contains no name, no email address and no game progress, and we don't store IP addresses with it. Reports are kept for up to 90 days and are used only to fix the game.

You can turn crash reports off at any time: Settings → Privacy & data → Send crash reports.

How Sentry handles data: https://sentry.io/privacy/
How Google handles Crashlytics data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Remote settings (iOS and Android apps)

The phone apps check Google Firebase Remote Config for a few switches we can change without an app update, for example to turn off an ad type or to tell you a new version is available. The check uses a random installation ID and no personal information.

Feedback

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. It includes the app version, your device type and any recent error messages. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted in the game and at its web address with a new date.
