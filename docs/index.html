Monkey See, Monkey Do

BETA

Watch the monkey dance, then copy the moves in the same order. Plays right in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.

Play now

Works offline after the first visit.

On a computer?

Point your phone's camera at this code to open the game on your phone.

Make it an app (optional)

  • iPhone / iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the game in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • It then opens full screen from its own icon and plays without internet.

What to try

  • Levels 1-3 teach the moves. Levels 2 and 3 are take turns: copy the monkey, add a move of your own, and the monkey copies you.
  • From level 4 every level is on a new map: beach, disco, moon, jungle.
  • The Wardrobe (skins and gear), Endless, Free Dance, and Settings > Graphics.
  • The Shop only sells in the phone apps. To try it here with pretend purchases (no money), use this test-mode link.

Good to know

  • Your progress is saved on this device, in this browser only. Another phone or browser starts fresh (Settings > Your data can copy a save code across).
  • No account, no ads, and nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
  • Tap once to start the sound. On iPhone, Settings > Sound > Play sound on silent decides what the silent switch does.
  • Older phone running slowly? Settings > Graphics > Low.
