# **COSMIC SAUCER DRIFT** · other Android stores

Do these after Google Play; they reuse the same build.

| Store | Status (Nov 2026) | Takes | Worth it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Samsung Galaxy Store** | Seller Portal; "commercial seller" status even for free apps (individuals OK) | APK or AAB (Samsung re-signs) | Medium |
| **Amazon Appstore** | Closed on ordinary Android phones on 20 Aug 2025; continues on **Fire tablets** (new Fire TV runs Vega OS, no APKs) | APK or AAB, ≤2.5 GB | Low–medium; test 3D speed on a Fire tablet |
| **Huawei AppGallery** | Free individual account with real-name ID; newer phones run HarmonyOS NEXT (no APKs) | APK/AAB | Low |

**Sideloading (itch.io APK, your website).** Google's developer verification starts with
installs from participating stores in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand (30 Sep 2026)
and reaches all certified phones in 2027. Register the package name and the APK's signing-key
SHA-256 in Play Console so off-store installs stay one tap. If Play generates your signing
key, an itch.io APK signed with another key can't update the Play install. Use your own key for
Play App Signing, or a separate package name off Play.
