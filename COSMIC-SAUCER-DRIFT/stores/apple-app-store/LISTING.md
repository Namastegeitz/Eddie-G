## App Review information (notes for the reviewer)
> Cosmic Saucer Drift is a complete offline arcade racer. No login or account is needed, and nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
>
> • Everything in single player works with touch: the joystick on the left steers (pull down to brake), DRIFT and ITEM are on the right. Tilt steering is optional in Settings.
> • Two-player split screen needs two game controllers (it's the only feature that does); it's under Play > Versus.
> • The privacy policy is linked in Settings > Privacy policy.
> • Quickest way to see a race: tap RACE NOW on the home screen.
