# **COSMIC SAUCER DRIFT** · store kits

One folder per store: what it costs, how long it takes, the exact rules, what we already
have on this computer, what's missing, the steps, and sources. Researched **17 Nov 2026**.

| Store | Cost | Fastest possible | Needs from you | Kit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| itch.io | Free | Same day | Account | [itch-io](itch-io/) |
| Newgrounds / Game Jolt | Free | Same day | Account | [web-portals](web-portals/) |
| Google Play | $25 once | ~17 days (14-day test), realistically 3–5 weeks | ID, Android 10+ phone, upload key, 12+ testers | [google-play](google-play/) |
| Steam | $100 per game | 30 days after paying | ID, tax, bank | [steam](steam/) |
| Apple App Store | $99 / year | Days–weeks (enrollment) + ~1 day review | ID, a Mac or cloud Mac | [apple-app-store](apple-app-store/) |
| Microsoft Store | Free | ~1–2 weeks | ID; a Windows PC for packaging | [microsoft-store](microsoft-store/) |
| Epic Games Store | $100 per game | ~3–5 weeks | ID, tax, payout | [epic-games-store](epic-games-store/) |
| Galaxy Store / Amazon Fire | Free | ~1 week | Seller account | [other-android-stores](other-android-stores/) |
| GOG | Free to apply | Curated: weeks–months | Apply after launching elsewhere | [gog](gog/) |

**Suggested order:** itch.io this week → open the Play account and pay the Steam fee
(both clocks start) → App Store → the optional stores → GOG.
