Cosmic Saucer Drift: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. Applies to every version: Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, Mac, Linux and the web.

Summary

Cosmic Saucer Drift does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The game works offline and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (Race Credits, Stardust, unlocked craft and pilots, upgrades, best times, time-trial ghosts, achievements and settings) on your own device: in the app's private storage on phones and tablets, in your user folder on a computer, and in your browser's storage on the web. It never leaves your device. You can erase it in Settings > Reset all progress, by uninstalling the game, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Permissions

On Android the game asks only for permission to vibrate, used for haptic feedback during races; you can turn vibration off in Settings. If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the motion sensor while you race; those readings are used only to steer and are never stored or sent. The iPhone, iPad, computer and web versions ask for no special permissions.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Stores

When you download the game from a store (Google Play, the App Store, Steam, itch.io and others), that store handles your purchase or download under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal data from them about you.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, saying what is collected, by whom, and how to opt out.

Contact

Questions: email the developer at the support address shown on the game's store page.
