Cosmic Saucer Drift: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. Applies to every version: Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, Mac, Linux and the web.

Summary

Cosmic Saucer Drift does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The game works offline. The only thing it can ever send is an anonymous crash report, and only if you say yes (see "Crash reports" below).

Crash reports (only if you agree)

Versions from the app stores and game stores can send a crash report when the game breaks, so we can fix it. The game asks you once; nothing is sent unless you choose Yes, and you can change your answer any time in Settings > Send crash reports. A report contains what went wrong (the error and where in the game's code it happened), your device model and operating system version, the game's version, short notes about what the game was doing (for example "race finished, place 2"), and a random installation number used to count how many players a crash affects. It contains no name, email address, location, contacts, photos, screenshots or advertising ID, and nothing you type.

Reports are processed for us by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) and by Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) as service providers, are sent encrypted, are not sold or shared with anyone else, and are not used for advertising or tracking. Crashlytics deletes them after 90 days; Sentry keeps them for the retention period of our plan. To ask us to delete reports from your device, email the support address on the store page. The web version never sends crash reports.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (Race Credits, Stardust, unlocked craft and pilots, upgrades, best times, time-trial ghosts, achievements and settings) on your own device: in the app's private storage on phones and tablets, in your user folder on a computer, and in your browser's storage on the web. It never leaves your device. You can erase it in Settings > Reset all progress, by uninstalling the game, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Permissions

On Android the game asks only for permission to vibrate, used for haptic feedback during races; you can turn vibration off in Settings. If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the motion sensor while you race; those readings are used only to steer and are never stored or sent. The iPhone, iPad, computer and web versions ask for no special permissions. Store versions with crash reports also have Android's internet permission, used only to send a report you agreed to.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Crash reports, if a parent or player turns them on, contain no personal information (see above).

Stores

When you download the game from a store (Google Play, the App Store, Steam, itch.io and others), that store handles your purchase or download under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal data from them about you.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, saying what is collected, by whom, and how to opt out.

Contact

Questions: email the developer at the support address shown on the game's store page.
