# Alien Sudoku: what's new

Written for players. Store "What's new" text comes from `store-launch/listing/listing.json`.

## 1.3.0 (Nov 28, 2026)
- **Smoother on every phone.** A new **Graphics** setting: Auto, Low, Medium, High or Ultra, plus resolution, frame rate, background motion and glow effects. Auto picks the right level for your phone and turns detail down by itself if the game starts to stutter.
- **Text size**: follows your phone's text size, or choose 100% to 150%.
- **Send feedback or report a bug** from Settings.
- **Crash reports** (you can turn them off in Settings) so problems get fixed quickly. They contain no personal information.
- **Rate the game** from Settings; the app may also ask, rarely, after a win.
- Glowing pulses on the campaign map and the Alien board use less battery.
- Security and stability updates (desktop app on Electron 45.5).

## 1.2.0 (Nov 18, 2026)
- Space in high definition: every backdrop redrawn at four times the detail, with framings made for phones held upright.
- The campaign is a star chart, and each sector has its own route map with nine stops.
- Finer detail on every board.

## 1.1.0 (Nov 18, 2026)
- Meet the **Star Cadets**: polite recruits raised on Earth's '80s and '90s broadcasts bring Medium puzzles in the Campaign, Daily and Quick Play, with their own song, Cadet Mixtape.
- Alien Creatures move to Easy in those modes.

## 1.0.0 (Nov 2026)
- First release: 54-level campaign, daily transmissions, Quick Play, Zen mode, Alien Duel against a computer rival, achievements, stats and a theme song for every difficulty.
