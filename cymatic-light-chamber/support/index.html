Support

Help with the app, the console and building the chamber.

Common questions

  • No sound? Press Start audio inside the console, turn your device volume up, and check the phone isn't on silent. Sound always fades in over 2 seconds.
  • The second tone sounds very quiet. That's the flicker guard: two tones that beat at 3-60 Hz would make the light flicker in the seizure-risk range, so the second tone plays at 10 %.
  • The pictures don't match my wall. Every picture in the app is a simulation. Real water depends on your dish, depth and amplifier; map your own with the console's pattern atlas.
  • Put it on my home screen. iPhone/iPad: Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu, Install app.
  • Stop everything now. Press FADE ALL (top right): sound fades to silence in 2 seconds.

Safety

24 V DC only near water, from a listed adapter on a GFCI outlet. Never connect the console's audio to a Tesla coil, and keep any Tesla coil at least 50 cm from the chamber. If you have epilepsy or light sensitivity, check with your clinician first.
